HydroMate privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

HydroMate is a Mac app that reminds you to drink water. It is made by Vinay Kanakuru Lakkappa (“I”). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: HydroMate collects nothing. Your data stays on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

HydroMate saves your daily goal, your reminder settings, the drinks you log and your break history on your Mac, inside the app’s own storage. This information is not sent to me or to anyone else.

Calendar (optional)

If you connect your calendar, HydroMate asks the Mac Calendar app only for the start and end times of busy events, to plan reminders around your meetings. It never reads, stores or sends event titles, guests, notes or locations. Busy times are only kept in memory while the app runs. Your reminder history on your Mac notes when each break was planned and whether it was planned around a meeting, never which meeting.

Microphone and camera

HydroMate checks only whether your microphone or camera is in use by another app, so it doesn’t interrupt your calls. It never turns them on, never records and never sees or hears anything.

Screen

HydroMate checks only whether another app is full screen (from the size of its windows and which app they belong to), so it doesn’t interrupt presentations or videos. It never reads what is on your screen or the names of your windows.

Keyboard and mouse

To know whether you’re away, HydroMate checks only how long ago the keyboard or mouse was last used. It never sees what you type or where you click.

Notifications

If you turn off the animated reminder, HydroMate shows Mac notifications instead. They are created on your Mac.

Export

If you export your data, HydroMate saves a file where you choose. What you do with that file is up to you.

No accounts, ads, tracking or analytics

This version has no accounts. HydroMate shows no ads, does not track you, and contains no analytics or advertising tools. If a later version adds an optional account, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Deleting your data

Quit HydroMate and move it to the Trash. To also remove its stored data, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.vinaykl.hydromate.mac.

Children

HydroMate collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
