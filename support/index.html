HydroMate support

HydroMate reminds you to drink water during your workday. Its mascot, Drip, walks onto your screen when it’s a good moment for a sip.

Drip didn’t come. Why?

Drip only comes during your reminder hours (Settings › Reminders › Work hours), and stays away while:

  • a meeting is in your calendar,
  • your microphone or camera is in use (for example in a call),
  • an app is full screen or a slideshow is running,
  • Presentation Mode is on,
  • your Mac is locked or you’re away,
  • reminders are paused (menu bar › Pause reminders).

To see Drip right now: Settings › Drip › Preview.

How do I connect my calendar?

Settings › Calendar › Connect Apple Calendar, then allow access when your Mac asks. HydroMate only checks when you’re busy or free.

My meetings are in Google or Outlook.

Add that account to the Mac Calendar app: open Calendar, choose Calendar › Add Account…, pick Google or Microsoft Exchange (for Outlook) and sign in. HydroMate then knows about those meetings too.

How do I start HydroMate automatically?

Settings › General › Launch at login.

How do I log a drink quickly?

Click the water drop in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥⌘W anywhere.

How do I stop reminders for a while?

Menu bar › Pause reminders, or turn on Presentation Mode while you present.

How do I remove HydroMate and its data?

Quit HydroMate, move it to the Trash, and delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.vinaykl.hydromate.mac.
